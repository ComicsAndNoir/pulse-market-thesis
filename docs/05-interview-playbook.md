# 05 · Interview playbook — running the demo live

The artifact is only half of it; how you run it is the other half. Same tool, very
different read depending on the order you reveal things.

## Open with the bet, not the tool

Say the sentence first, out loud, before sharing your screen:

> "If I were the first PM on Pulse, I'd point it at churn/exit interviews first. Here's the
> reasoning that got me there — and here's what would change my mind."

Then bring up the board as the **defense** of that sentence. If you open with the tool, you
look like you're presenting a framework. If you open with the bet and use the tool to
withstand pressure, you look like you're presenting judgment.

## Walk the rubric — and flag the counterintuitive weight

Point at the weights and say why proof-speed and ROI are highest, then flag the move that
signals experience: **you deliberately underweighted TAM/expansion.** A 0-1 product with no
users dies from not reaching the buyer and not proving value fast — not from a small market.

## The set-piece: turn an objection into a proof point

When someone pushes — *"why isn't the huge 1:1 market your pick?"* — don't argue. Drag the
**Expansion Path** weight up in front of them and narrate:

> "Even if I weight expansion far higher, watch — 1:1s climbs, but it still loses, because
> it has no single buyer and no ROI you can price. Churn stays on top."

Then hit **Reset** to return to your recommended weighting. That interaction *is* the
interview.

## Land the kill list

Scroll to the kill list unprompted. Naming what Pulse is **not** — and exactly why — is the
Kill Discipline competency, and almost nobody brings it. The **Weekly 1:1s** kill is the
headline: the biggest, most obvious market is the trap.

## Defend the structure, never the numbers

Everyone knows the scores are illustrative. If you defend the exact numbers you look naive.
Defend the **weights and the structure**. The composite is a conversation-starter, not an
answer — say that.

## If asked "how did you build this so fast?"

Name the AI-native workflow and the ~overnight timeline. That directly checks the
"AI-First Operator — shipped something with AI in the last ninety days" box, in the room.

## Close

Offer the live URL and the shareable weighting link. "I'll send the board weighted the way
we discussed" is a strong last impression and reinforces the whole thesis.
